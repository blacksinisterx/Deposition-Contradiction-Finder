DEPOSITION OF DAVID KESSLER — VOLUME I
Martinez v. Coastal Freight Co.
Taken March 3, 2026

[4:02] Q. Mr. Kessler, please state your position at Coastal Freight.
[4:04] A. I'm a warehouse loader. Been there about six years.
[4:07] Q. And on the morning of January 14th, where were you working?
[4:09] A. Bay 3, same as always. Loading pallets onto the trucks.
[4:12] Q. Were you familiar with the forklift involved in the accident, unit 12?
[4:14] A. Yeah, I'd used it myself plenty of times.
[4:16] Q. Was anything unusual about unit 12 that morning, before the accident?
[4:18] A. The hydraulic warning light was on when Tom got on it. I remember thinking someone should log it before he took it out.
[4:22] Q. Did you say anything to him about that?
[4:24] A. No, I figured it wasn't my place. He'd been driving longer than me.

[5:01] Q. Where were you standing at the moment the accident occurred?
[5:03] A. I was standing right next to the forklift when it happened. Maybe six feet away.
[5:06] Q. Can you describe what you saw?
[5:08] A. The forks were raised too high for the load, and it just tipped forward. Happened fast.
[5:12] Q. Did you see what caused the load to shift?
[5:14] A. Not exactly. I heard a bang first, then it was already going over.

[8:01] Q. Let's talk about the safety guard on unit 12. Are you aware it was found removed after the accident?
[8:03] A. I heard that afterward, yeah.
[8:05] Q. Did you see anyone remove the safety guard from unit 12 that morning?
[8:07] A. No, I did not see anyone remove it.
[8:09] Q. So as far as you know, when was it removed?
[8:11] A. I have no idea. It must have already been off when I got there that morning. I never saw it on unit 12, not that day.
[8:15] Q. You're certain of that?
[8:17] A. As certain as I can be. I didn't see it removed.
